Lab rules

Five rules every experiment here follows. If a team adopts nothing else, adopt these.

  • Nothing grades its own output. The reviewer is a different model, and the harness decides who it is.
  • Every score cites evidence. A grade with no fact behind it gets lowered until it has one.
  • Cheap checks run first. Plain, model-free tests run on every change. Expensive judges only look at what is new.
  • A human outranks the automation. A person checks what the machines cannot, and wins any disagreement.
  • Say what was not measured. "Estimate" and "one run" stay attached to the number.
